# Eligibility notes (PCT)

Not a search, not a written opinion, and not legal advice. The
International Searching Authority applies novelty, inventive step, and
industrial applicability (PCT Article 33). A national office applies its
own law again at national phase. This checklist only decides whether
drafting an English international specification is a rational next step.

## C. Things to mark, not to draft

- Sequence listing (WIPO ST.26) if the office will require one. Do not invent residues.
- Deposit of biological material if the invention cannot be enabled without a deposit. Do not invent an accession number.
- Genetic-resource source disclosure for a later CN national phase (专利法 Art. 26).
