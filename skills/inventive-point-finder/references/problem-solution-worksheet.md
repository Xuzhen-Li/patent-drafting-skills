## 5. PCT Article 33

Skip with `not requested` for a pure CN or pure US file.

Inventive step on the international standard: not obvious to a person
skilled in the art having regard to the prior art. Use §§ 1–2. Note any
national phase (CN Art. 22 or US § 103) that will need a different write-up.
Do not claim that a future written opinion will be positive.
