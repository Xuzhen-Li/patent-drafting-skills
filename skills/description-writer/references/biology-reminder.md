# Biology reminder / 生物技术提醒

This is a reminder for counsel. It is not a completed legal form, a
sequence listing, or a deposit receipt. Do not clear the issue by changing
the title.

Copy the unanswered items into `05-description/domain_review.md`.

## Genetic-resource source / 遗传资源来源披露

专利法 Art. 26, paragraph 5: where the invention is completed by relying
on genetic resources (遗传资源), the applicant states the direct source
and the original source in the application. If the original source cannot
be stated, the applicant states the reasons.

实施细则 (2023 revision) Art. 29 defines 遗传资源 and says the applicant
explains this in the request and fills the office form. A PCT application
entering the China national phase states the same reliance under 实施细则
Art. 126. Do not invent a source country, a provider, or a collection date.

Ask the inventor only: did this invention use the genetic function of
material from a human, animal, plant, or microorganism? If yes, list what
they already know and mark the rest `【待发明人确认】`.

## Sequence listing / 序列表

If the disclosure contains nucleotide or amino-acid sequences, a listing
under WIPO ST.26 is for counsel or WIPO's sequence tool. ST.26 has applied
to filings since 1 July 2022. Confirm the version in force on the filing
date before generating the XML.

Do not generate, complete, translate, or "clean up" a sequence. If the
inventor has not supplied the sequence, write that the listing cannot be
prepared.

## Deposit / 生物材料保藏

实施细则 Art. 27: when the invention involves a new biological material
the public cannot obtain, and the description is not enough for a skilled
person to carry it out, the applicant deposits a sample with a depository
recognized by the office and files the deposit and viability documents.
The request and the description then name the taxonomy (Latin name), the
depository, its address, the deposit date, and the accession number.

Do not invent an accession number. Many recognized depositories are
international depositary authorities under the Budapest Treaty. Counsel
confirms which depository and whether a deposit is required.

PCT applicants who already explained a deposit under the Treaty still
check the national-phase rules. 实施细则 Art. 125 is the China national-phase
provision. Do not treat a PCT sentence as a completed CN deposit.
