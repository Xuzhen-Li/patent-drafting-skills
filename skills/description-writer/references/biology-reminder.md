# Biology reminder

This is a reminder for counsel. It is not a completed legal form, a
sequence listing, or a deposit receipt. Do not clear the issue by changing
the title.

Copy the unanswered items into `05-description/domain_review.md`.

## Genetic-resource source

Some offices require the applicant to state where genetic resources came
from when the invention relies on them. China does, and a later China
national phase of a PCT application is covered by that rule. This English
suite does not draft that statement. Ask the inventor only: did this
invention use the genetic function of material from a human, animal,
plant, or microorganism? If yes, list what they already know, mark the
rest `[INVENTOR TO CONFIRM]`, and flag it for counsel. Do not invent a
source country, a provider, or a collection date.

## Sequence listing

If the disclosure contains nucleotide or amino-acid sequences, a listing
under WIPO ST.26 is for counsel or WIPO's sequence tool. ST.26 has applied
to filings since 1 July 2022. Confirm the version in force on the filing
date before generating the XML.

Do not generate, complete, translate, or "clean up" a sequence. If the
inventor has not supplied the sequence, write that the listing cannot be
prepared.

## Deposit

When the invention involves biological material that the public cannot
obtain and the description alone does not let a skilled person carry it
out, a deposit may be needed. US rules: 37 CFR 1.801 and following (MPEP
chapter 2400; MPEP 2404 covers when a deposit is needed). Many recognized
depositories are international depositary authorities under the Budapest
Treaty. Counsel confirms which depository and whether a deposit is
required.

Do not invent an accession number. PCT applicants who already explained a
deposit still check each national phase's rules.
