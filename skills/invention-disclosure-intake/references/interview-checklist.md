# Interview checklist

Ask these in the project language. Record the inventor's words. If they
do not know, write the unconfirmed marker. Do not supply a typical value
from the field.

The blank form in `00-disclosure/disclosure_form.md` is the document you
are filling. This checklist is the order of questions, not a second template.

## Pass A — problem

1. In one sentence, what is the product, process, or composition?
2. What technical field should a reader use to classify it?
3. What goes wrong with the usual approach, under what conditions?
4. Which products, papers, or patents have you actually used or read?
   What is the specific defect of each (yield, stability, false positive,
   step count, fouling, whatever you measured or observed)?
5. Which of those defects is this invention meant to remove?

## Pass B — solution and proof

6. List the parts, steps, or components in the order a skilled person
   would build or run them.
7. For each item: is it essential, or only a preference? What happens if
   it is omitted?
8. Which numbers are already measured or fixed (temperature, concentration,
   size, time, pH, voltage, cultivar, model number)? Which are still open?
9. What result did you see, against which control, on which date or in
   which notebook or figure? If you have not run it, say "not run".
   Grade each effect `measured`, `experienced`, `directly-inferable`,
   or `unverified`.
10. What else did you try that failed?
11. What could be swapped (material, order, instrument), and did you try the swap?
12. Which figures can you provide, and which reference signs do you want?

## Pass C — people and disclosure

13. List papers, preprints, theses, posters, talks, sales, samples,
    exhibitions, web pages, and public repositories, with dates. Include
    scheduled disclosures that have not happened yet.
14. Was any disclosure against your wishes by someone else? When?
15. Who conceived which feature? Who only collected data or wrote the paper?
16. Who should the applicant be? Is this a service invention under an
    employment or university policy you still need to check?
17. Genetic resources, human or clinical data you cannot publish, or a
    sequence that may need a sequence listing?
18. Is there anything you do not want in the broadest claim concept?

## Origin of each fact

For every feature and effect, record one origin in `fact_ledger.md`:
`user-confirmed`, `copied-from-file`, `summarized`, `suggested`, or
`open`. If you proposed the feature, the tag is `suggested` until the
inventor adopts it. Do not leave the origin column blank.

## Lock

Read back the essential-feature list and the disclosure table. Ask for
`发明人确认锁定交底` or `I confirm the invention lock` only when sections
4, 5, and 11 of the form are no longer empty placeholders.
