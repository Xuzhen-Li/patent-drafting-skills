# Glossary — Chinese and English patent terms

Working vocabulary for this suite. CN 实用性 is "practical applicability"
under 专利法 Art. 22 (the invention can be made or used and can produce a
positive effect). PCT and the EPC use "industrial applicability" for a
related but not identical test. Both appear below so a translation does not
collapse them.

"Said" is the traditional English equivalent of 所述. Current US claims more
often use "the", with the same antecedent-basis rule.
